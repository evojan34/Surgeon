## الخلاصة السريرية المركزة (High-Yield Pearls)
* **المعيار الذهبي للأمان:** تحقيق *Critical View of Safety (CVS)* وفق وصف *Strasberg* قبل قطع أي بنية تشريحية.
* **حدود مثلث كالوت (Calot's Triangle):** محصور بين القناة الكيسية (سفلياً)، القناة الكبدية المشتركة (إنسياً)، والحافة السفلية للكبد (علوياً).
* **معلم الأمان الخلفي:** التلم الكبدي *Rouvière’s sulcus* الذي يحدد مستوى الصفيحة الكبدية الحشوية بدقة.

## سؤال الامتحان المقالي للبورد (Board Essay Scenario)

> **Scenario:** A 48-year-old female presents to the Emergency Department with a 72-hour history of severe right upper quadrant pain, fever (38.8°C), and leukocytosis ($16,500/\mu\text{L}$). Ultrasound reveals a distended gallbladder with an impacted stone in the neck, wall thickening of 6 mm, and pericholecystic fluid.

**المطلوب في ورقة الإجابة:**
1. Outline your initial management plan and resuscitation goals. *(20 Marks)*
2. Detail your operative strategy for laparoscopic cholecystectomy in this "difficult gallbladder", explicitly describing the three mandatory criteria for the **Critical View of Safety (CVS)**. *(50 Marks)*
3. If CVS cannot be achieved due to dense inflammatory "frozen" tissue, what "Bailout Options" should you choose? Justify according to *Bailey & Love 28th*. *(30 Marks)*

---

## سلم الدرجات النموذجي (Marking Rubric)

### 1. Initial Management (20 Marks)
* NPO, IV fluid resuscitation guided by urine output (>0.5 mL/kg/h). (5m)
* Early IV broad-spectrum antibiotics covering Gram-negatives and anaerobes (Tokyo Guidelines 2018). (5m)
* Analgesia (NSAIDs/Opioids) and thromboembolic prophylaxis. (5m)
* Timing of surgery: Early laparoscopic cholecystectomy within the index admission (preferably within 72h). (5m)

### 2. Operative Strategy & CVS Criteria (50 Marks)
* Proper port placement with lateral and cephalic fundic traction. (10m)
* Identification of *Rouvière’s sulcus* as the safe transverse line. (10m)
* **The 3 Mandatory CVS Criteria (30m):**
  1. Complete clearance of the hepatocystic triangle from fat and fibrous tissue.
  2. Dissection of the lower third of the gallbladder off the liver bed (cystic plate exposure).
  3. Strict visualization of only TWO structures entering the gallbladder (cystic duct and cystic artery).

### 3. Bailout Strategies (30 Marks)
* Stop hazardous dissection to prevent vasculobiliary injury (BDI). (5m)
* **Subtotal Cholecystectomy:**
  * Fenestrating vs. Reconstituting technique (B & L recommendation). (15m)
* Cholecystostomy tube placement if patient is unstable. (5m)
* Conversion to open surgery or calling for a senior colleague. (5m)
